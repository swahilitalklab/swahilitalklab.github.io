What's at risk

Most deployment failures are invisible
until the relationship is already broken.

  • Silence in meetings is not confusion — it carries precise relational meaning that most outsiders misread entirely
  • Trust is built through protocol, not performance — skipping early relationship-building creates barriers that persist for months
  • Hierarchy signalling differs sharply from Western norms — misjudging seniority cues damages credibility before work begins
  • Direct disagreement is rarely voiced — undetected misalignment accumulates quietly into serious operational risk
  • Role introduction in the wrong register — even brief language choices — sets the wrong expectations for the entire engagement
