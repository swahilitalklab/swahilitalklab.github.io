For professionals working in, preparing to work in, or leading multicultural teams across East Africa.

Submit your question Or join the next conversation

What you can bring

  • Something happened at work and I'm not sure how to interpret it.
  • I'm facing a recurring communication or team challenge.
  • I understand the Swahili words, but not what they mean in this context.
  • I'm preparing to work in East Africa and want to think a situation through before I arrive.
  • I suspect culture is part of the issue, but I'm not sure.

You don't have to know whether something is cultural. Bring the situation, and we can examine it together.

How it works

  1. Submit a questionSend it before the conversation so it can be considered in advance.
  2. Join the monthly conversationWe explore selected questions and the situations behind them.
  3. Or simply listen inYou can join without a question and learn from the ones others bring.
